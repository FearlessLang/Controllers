= 1. A VM round starts on a desk that is ready =

On a freshly booted fedora_gnome the manager is the first X11 client: Xwayland starts cold with it and the window

= 3. Desks with neither tray nor minimization (void_i3) =

With no StatusNotifierWatcher the manager iconifies on close (Tray calls window.iconifyOnClose()). i3 ignores
iconify requests, so on void_i3 the close does nothing visible, and nothing can bring a hidden window back.
Proposal: on a desk with no tray the manager draws its own tray substitute in Swing: a small undecorated
always-on-top window with the Fearless icon sitting in a screen corner. Closing the main window hides it and
shows the corner icon; a click on the icon reopens the window; a right click opens the same menu as the tray
icon. This takes full control and behaves the same on every desk with no tray (debian_mate, debian_gnome_x11,
fedora_gnome, void_i3), so iconifyOnClose may go away.
Open points: which corner (a corner a panel does not cover), whether tiling desks float an undecorated
always-on-top window (i3 floats dialogs and owned windows), and how WindowCloseTest checks it.
